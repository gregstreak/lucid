What Lucid is

Lucid is a migraine tracker. It records what you log and, when you ask for one, produces a written analysis of patterns in your own data. It is not medical advice. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace a doctor.

What stays on your device

Lucid has no accounts. Everything you enter is stored in your browser, on your device:

  • your profile: name, age range, biological sex, how long you have had migraines, how often, triggers and history notes
  • your daily logs, migraine events and supplements
  • your saved analyses

We hold no copy of this. We cannot see it and we cannot recover it. If you clear your browser’s data or lose your device, it is gone unless you have saved a backup (Settings, Back up your entries). A backup file holds your health entries, so keep it somewhere private.

Health information

Information about your health is special personal information under POPIA. We process it only with your consent. It leaves your device only when you run an analysis, and Lucid asks for your consent before the first one.

When you run an analysis

Lucid sends a summary of your data through our server to Anthropic, which runs the AI model that writes the analysis. The summary holds:

  • your age range, biological sex, years with migraines, self-reported frequency, triggers and history notes (the notes are free text, so keep them general)
  • the names and doses of your supplements, and the medications you logged
  • figures and dates from your logs: sleep, water, swimming, migraine counts, severity, onset times and clusters of migraines

It does not hold your name or your email address.

Our server passes the summary on and stores nothing. Anthropic processes it outside South Africa, including in the United States. Under Anthropic’s standard terms it deletes the inputs and outputs of its API within 30 days. It may keep them longer where the law requires or to enforce its usage policy. Anthropic’s own terms apply to what it holds.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings (Withdraw analysis consent). Lucid then asks again before the next analysis. Information Anthropic has already received stays subject to its own retention period.

Your location

When Lucid opens, it asks your device for your location so that it can show barometric pressure. If you allow it, your coordinates go to Open-Meteo, which supplies the weather data, and to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service, which supplies the name of your town. Lucid rounds the coordinates to about one kilometre before it sends them. We do not store your location. If you refuse permission, the rest of Lucid works and the pressure display is unavailable.

Payment

Paid analysis costs R149 once, through Paystack. At the unlock screen you enter an email address, which goes to Paystack for your receipt. You enter your card details on Paystack’s own page, and they never reach us. Paystack keeps its own records of the payment, as the law requires.

Lucid keeps the payment reference on your device so that it can confirm you have paid. If you ask for a refund, we use the email you paid with and the reference to find the payment in Paystack. Pricing, refunds and contact details are on our policies page.

Other services your device contacts

To display Lucid, your device loads fonts from Google Fonts and the PDF library from Cloudflare (cdnjs) or jsDelivr. Those providers see your IP address when it does. Our hosting provider, Vercel, records technical details of requests, including IP addresses, to deliver the service and to limit repeated requests.

Outside South Africa

Anthropic, Vercel, Paystack, Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap, Google and Cloudflare may process information outside South Africa. We say “may” because we have not checked where each of them keeps it.

Cookies

Lucid sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for your entries and your access token. Paystack may set its own cookies on its payment page.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising or send marketing emails.

How long we keep it

We keep none of your health entries, because we never receive them. Anthropic’s retention is described above. Paystack and your bank keep payment records as the law requires. Information on your device stays there until you delete it.

Your rights

Because we hold no copy of your entries, you control them already: you can change or delete them in the app, and you can clear them by clearing your browser’s data. For anything we do hold, such as our correspondence with you, you may ask what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email us at the address above.

If you think we have handled your information unlawfully, you may complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

Lucid is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
